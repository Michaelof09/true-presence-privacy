True Presence privacy

Effective August 27, 2026

Location

True Presence uses your device location while the app is open to find a nearby mapped Catholic church. It sends a rounded location to public OpenStreetMap/Overpass services, calculates distance and direction on the device, and does not run location tracking in the background.

What we keep

The app has no account, ads, analytics, location history, or app server. It stores only the last church result on your device for a nearby offline fallback. Delete the app to remove that local data. You can turn off location access in iPhone Settings.

Other services

When you tap Open in Maps, Apple Maps receives the destination. Apple's privacy policy applies to that service. OpenStreetMap/Overpass is a public third-party service; its own logs and privacy rules apply to requests it receives.

Map limits

Map data may be incomplete or wrong. True Presence does not verify church hours, parish status, or tabernacle access.
